# Evidence for the NSE daily trading desk

Reviewed November 19, 2026. The application implements these setups as research hypotheses for liquid cash equities. Record the rule and validate material changes before interpreting its results as evidence of an edge.

## Session and data

- **09:00–09:15 IST.** Pre-open includes order collection, indicative equilibrium values, matching, and a buffer. A 09:00 brief can use dated disclosures and the prior final cash-market prices while waiting for regular-session bars. The regular NSE cash session opens at 09:15. Its normal end is 15:30, with a 15:15 continuous-trading cutoff for eligible closing-auction securities under the November 2026 arrangements. [NSE pre-open](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-market-pre-open) · [NSE market timings](https://betanseapi.nseindia.com/static/market-data/market-timings) · [NSE closing auction](https://betanseapi.nseindia.com/static/products-services/closing-auction-session)
- **15:00 IST simulated time exit.** This leaves a buffer ahead of November's closing auction and the earlier of the published broker cutoffs. Zerodha publishes 15:12 for closing-auction cash stocks and 15:25 for other cash stocks. Groww's risk policy specifies 15:05 for F&O-enabled cash stocks and 15:20 for other cash stocks. NSE's CAS 2.0 effective December 7, 2026 moves the cash-auction transition to 15:30. [Zerodha square-off](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/trading-faqs/market-sessions/articles/intraday-auto-square-off-timings) · [Groww risk policy](https://groww.in/p/risk-policy) · [NSE CAS 2.0 FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/mediaattachment/2026-11/FAQs_for_CM_and_FO_FOR_CAS_2.0_20261109152618.pdf)
- **Calendar and exchange restrictions.** Watch weekends, cash-market holidays, special sessions, market halts, security suspensions, and trading-series changes. Trade-for-trade and auction-only securities can violate continuous short-horizon fill assumptions. Session dates and timestamps use `Asia/Kolkata`. [NSE 2026 capital-market holiday circular](https://archives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR71775.pdf) · [NSE surveillance actions](https://betanseapi.nseindia.com/regulations/exchange-market-surveillance-actions) · [NSE Graded Surveillance Measure](https://betanseapi.nseindia.com/static/regulations/graded-surveillance-measure)
- **Source timestamp.** Track the candle time, the fetch time, and the bar interval. A candle stamped with its start becomes eligible only after its entire interval has elapsed. Missing opening bars, feed delays, and gaps in recent price data downgrade the signal. Previous daily closes and corporate-action adjustment conventions must be compatible with the opening price before computing a gap. A public candle supports chart inspection and replay. An execution-quality study also needs contemporaneous quotes and spreads. ## Experimental setups

| Setup | Condition and rationale | Main failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening range breakout | Use the first three regular-session five-minute bars for the 15-minute range. Look for a new close above the range high and session VWAP, with same-window relative volume at least 1.5×. | Thin opening liquidity, a brief sweep of the range high, or chasing beyond the entry cap. |
| VWAP reclaim | Look for a completed bar to reclaim a flat or rising session VWAP after a pullback with relative volume at least 1.2×. The local pullback provides an observable invalidation area. | Repeated crossings in a flat market and sudden price gaps that make the apparent stop unreliable. |
| Gap continuation | Require a regular-session open 0.8–3% above the compatible previous close, then a range break above VWAP with relative volume at least 1.5×. Check for ex-dates, split adjustments, and identified catalysts. | The previous close can be stale or incompatible with the open. A valid gap can also reverse sharply. |

Approximate session VWAP as the volume-weighted average of each completed bar's `(high + low + close) / 3`, starting at the regular open. Compare the current bar's volume with prior sessions at the same clock time. Opening and closing activity tend to be higher in NSE data, so comparing the opening bar with an all-session average exaggerates its activity. Show the bar timestamp, range levels, VWAP and volume confirmation next to each setup. [Zerodha Varsity VWAP](https://zerodha.com/varsity/chapter/supplementary-notes-1/) · [Gopalaswamy and Sampath NSE study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0972652720930586) · [Groww setup and position-sizing guide](https://groww.in/blog/intraday-trading-strategies) · [Groww chart-level controls](https://groww.in/blog/trade-on-charts-on-groww)

The original Zarattini, Barbon, and Aziz study tested five-minute opening-range breaks and opening-volume filters in US equities during 2016–2023. It tested long and short trades with different sizing and leverage. Its results provide a hypothesis for this desk's 15-minute NSE long-only rules. An India-specific DataPointX study adapted two different US intraday rules to NIFTY 50 stocks. Its noise-band gross edge fell to 1.5 basis points in 2024–2026 and both strategies lost after the study's estimated costs. This is direct evidence that US results and plausible indicators can fail to survive Indian trading costs. [Original opening-range paper](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/3c2989c4-688d-4d78-8a71-f02690990d51/content) · [DataPointX's Indian replication](https://datapointx.com/research/in/academic-strategies-in-india/)

## Sizing and validation

- **Cash and stop scenario.** Whole-share size must fit the cash budget including estimated entry friction, and the stop scenario must fit the desk's 0.65% risk cap for that setup. At ₹5,000 that cap is ₹32.50. Select the largest quantity consistent with both limits. An estimated stop loss includes the entry-to-stop move and modelled round-trip costs. Zero affordable shares means wait. Choices are individual scenarios and use the same available budget. [Zerodha position sizing](https://zerodha.com/varsity/chapter/position-sizing/) · [Zerodha cost schedule](https://zerodha.com/charges) · [Groww cost schedule](https://groww.in/pricing)
- **Friction.** Zerodha lists 0.03% brokerage capped at ₹20 per executed cash-intraday order, 0.025% STT on sells, and stamp duty on buys. Exchange and SEBI charges apply to turnover, and GST applies to the broker and applicable charges. Groww has a different brokerage schedule with a capped minimum. An estimate for one broker can misstate another broker's costs. Spread, slippage, rejected orders, and gaps over a stop change realised outcomes. SEBI found that 71% of sampled individual equity-cash intraday traders made net losses in FY23. That cohort rate is context for transaction costs and cannot estimate the chance of loss on a particular setup. [Zerodha cost schedule](https://zerodha.com/charges) · [Groww intraday fees](https://groww.in/help/stocks/sx-pricing/what-are-intraday-charges) · [SEBI cash intraday study](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jul-2024/1721818140715.pdf)
- **Replay discipline.** Preserve each original observation and use only bars completed before a signal. Process the next bar's open in the bounded range for a modelled entry. Record a miss when the actual next bar opens outside the range. Use the worse stop when a candle touches both stop and target and intrabar sequencing is unknown. Use a gap-through price when a bar opens below a long stop. A last observed price during a halt or feed outage does not prove anyone could exit there. [QuantConnect fill modelling](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/reality-modeling/trade-fills/key-concepts) · [QuantConnect slippage modelling](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/reality-modeling/slippage/key-concepts)
- **Measure before publishing probabilities.** Freeze the rule and use walk-forward validation with a final unseen period and a point-in-time universe. Include delistings, filings available at the time, costs, adverse spread and latency, missed fills, halts, and same-timestamp signal competition for capital. Report outcomes with counts, period, benchmark and out-of-sample net expectancy. Show cost sensitivity and uncertainty and compare against inactive or simple reference rules. The setup's stop-distance tier is a risk descriptor. Any win-rate estimate needs the version, observation window, sample count and an uncertainty range. [Freqtrade lookahead analysis](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/lookahead-analysis/) · [LEAN engine](https://github.com/QuantConnect/Lean) · [vectorbt simulation implementation](https://github.com/polakowo/vectorbt/blob/master/vectorbt/portfolio/base.py)

## Company research and public discussion

Start with the exchange announcement or results filing. Record when the announcement reached the exchange and distinguish the filing time, the reporting period, and any record, ex-, meeting, or effective date. In a results release, look for consolidated revenue, profit before and after exceptional items, margin or provision changes, and company-stated guidance. In a corporate action or deal, determine the effective date and whether it is proposed, approved, or completed. Use a present-day result only once it was published.
